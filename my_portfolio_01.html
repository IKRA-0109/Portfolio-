About Me

I am a first-year B.Sc. Forensic Science student at Lovely Professional University, developing my understanding of scientific investigation, evidence examination, and the principles that support forensic laboratory work.

I am particularly interested in forensic biology and DNA analysis because they connect biology and genetics with the scientific examination of evidence. I want to understand how laboratory procedures, accurate documentation, quality control, and scientific reasoning contribute to reliable forensic findings.

As I progress through my degree, I aim to strengthen my foundational knowledge, gain practical exposure, and explore different forensic disciplines before deciding on a final specialisation.
